## Anti-patterns

- **"Natural community count → that many axes" is a category error.** When a notebook-author motivates a 4+-axis hive plot by "the data naturally has N communities," that motivates the *partition variable*, not the *radial layout*. The corpus answer for partition with N>3 is `HivePlotMatrix.from_partition` (or two-layer axes, or group-collapse), not axes around a single origin. `examples/hive_plots_more_than_three_groups.ipynb` cell 29 calls flat 4+-axis layouts "particularly disingenuous, as it implies at first glance that we are looking at the full network, when in fact we are missing roughly half of the [inter-axis] connections." Swapping to `from_partition` instead yields an N×N upper-triangular matrix whose diagonal cells hold repeat axes for one group and whose off-diagonal cells hold two named groups plus a collapsed "Other" axis.

## Gotchas

- **Repeat axes without two-tone coloring is undifferentiated spaghetti, but only on standalone `HivePlot`.** When `repeat_axes=True` is set but `repeat_edge_kwargs` / `non_repeat_edge_kwargs` are not, intra-group (repeat) and inter-group (non-repeat) edges are visually indistinguishable (skill hive-plot rule 1); in review, check the rendered figure for color contrast between the two edge classes, not just kwarg presence. **Exempt: `HivePlotMatrix` cells.** With `from_partition` + `include_diagonal=True` (default), the matrix structure does the differentiation (diagonal cells intra-group, off-diagonal inter-group), so no two-tone coloring is needed or available, and `repeat_axes=True` should be dropped from the call.
- **When a caption names specific edges as its subject, those edges must be visually marked, and instructional-tier calibration does not exempt the marking.** A before/after intra-group figure whose caption says "the recovered intra-group edges are the ones curving between each axis and its repeat" must distinguish those edges from the rest, or the caption points at nothing. The silhouette change alone (3-axis to pinwheel) sells "coverage rose" but not which curves are the recovered ones. This is load-bearing encoding, not polish: a ~3-line `update_edges(partition_id_1=ax, partition_id_2=ax, ...)` loop distinguishing the named edges is well under the matplotlib budget. Flag the absence of any distinction, `worth-discussing` (the headline still reads); the palette and linewidth are the author's call, so do not push a specific color scheme, and check the rendered figure since existing kwargs may already distinguish them.
- **Title `y` is render-aspect-dependent.** Two cells using identical `y=0.75` can render with very different whitespace above the figure body when aspect ratios differ (e.g. colorbar presence/absence affects figure height). Do not flag a deviation from a sibling figure's `y` without looking at the rendered output first.
- **On a `from_variable_sweep` partition-sweep, `unify_axes=True` is right and a cell compressing toward the origin is the honest signal, not a collapse.** Skill hive-plot rule 4 frames unify around cells that share axes; the partition-sweep is a distinct config where each cell has *different* axes but a *shared sort variable*, so unify scales every axis to that metric's global range and radial position then means the same sort-metric value across cells (the cross-cell comparability the sweep exists to show). A partition mixing the metric within groups (e.g. `community`) clusters near the origin while `qcut` *tier* partitions fill the spoke; that contrast is the readable payoff and is destroyed under autoscale. Don't recommend autoscale or dissent from the compression; confirm the compressed cell still resolves individual nodes/spokes (it did at 77 nodes) rather than blobbing.
- **At exactly 3 groups, reach for a single 3-axis `HivePlot` with repeat axes, not `HivePlotMatrix`.** Three axes make every group pair adjacent and the repeats carry intra-group edges, so one panel is complete. The matrix instead gives off-diagonal cells of two named groups plus a collapsed "Other", which at 3 groups is the lone third group, so every cell re-admits all three bridges. A gate concurred with an "each cell localizes one pair" claim on exactly that figure; the post-impl pass caught it and the matrix was cut from the notebook.
- **Judge render fidelity from standalone figures at production size, never from a grid of variants.** Packing curve-resolution variants into one comparison figure shrinks every panel, so at fixed DPI each curve lands in fewer pixels and the faceting it is supposed to expose hides. A 2026-08-02 grid read as "indistinguishable at `num_steps=16`, and 8 looks pretty good"; re-rendered one plot per figure at the real resolution, 8 was visibly choppy. The maintainer called it in advance: shrinking a blurry image makes it look better than it is. Flag any fidelity or equivalence claim sourced from a multi-panel comparison.
- **Pre-existing row/col label string-coercion in `HivePlotMatrix.from_partition` is cosmetic, not blocking.** `hiveplot_matrix.py:1220` does `group_labels = [str(g) for g in partition_values]`, so integer community labels (e.g. from `louvain_communities`) render as strings `'0'`, `'1'`, etc. in row/col positions. Confusion risk is low (matplotlib auto-stringifies any non-string label anyway), so flag `worth-discussing` if it surfaces in a v0.28+ review; do not block a Louvain-workflow ship. The one-line fix (`group_labels = list(partition_values)`) warrants its own dispatch with regression-test coverage because it touches every `from_partition` output.
