## Anti-patterns

- **"Natural community count → that many axes" is a category error.** When a notebook-author motivates a 4+-axis hive plot by "the data naturally has N communities," check what that motivates: it motivates the *partition variable*, not the *radial layout*. The corpus canonical answer for partition with N>3 is `HivePlotMatrix.from_partition` (or two-layer axes, or group-collapse), not adding axes around a single origin. `examples/hive_plots_more_than_three_groups.ipynb` cell 29 calls flat 4+-axis layouts "particularly disingenuous, as it implies at first glance that we are looking at the full network, when in fact we are missing roughly half of the [inter-axis] connections." Anchor: viz-critic review of Workstream E on `examples/computing_graph_metrics.ipynb` (Karate Louvain 4-axis, Les Mis Louvain 6-axis flagged must-fix); the amendment closed cleanly by swapping in `HivePlotMatrix.from_partition`, producing 4x4 and 6x6 upper-triangular matrices of 2-axis pairwise cells.

## Gotchas

- **Repeat axes without two-tone coloring is undifferentiated spaghetti, but only on standalone `HivePlot`.** When `repeat_axes=True` is set on a `HivePlot` but `repeat_edge_kwargs` / `non_repeat_edge_kwargs` are not, intra-group (repeat) and inter-group (non-repeat) edges are visually indistinguishable. The skill hive-plot rule 1 names this; in review, check the rendered figure for color contrast between the two edge classes, not just the kwarg presence. **Exempt: `HivePlotMatrix` cells.** When the matrix is built via `HivePlotMatrix.from_partition` with `include_diagonal=True` (default), the matrix structure itself does the inter-vs-intra differentiation: diagonal cells are intra-group, off-diagonal cells are inter-group. No two-tone edge coloring is needed (or even available, since `include_diagonal=True` handles the diagonal-repeats automatically and `repeat_axes=True` should be dropped from the call). Anchor: same review as the anti-pattern above; the original 4- and 6-axis `HivePlot` figures hit the gotcha, the amendment HPMs do not.

- **Title `y` is render-aspect-dependent.** Two cells using identical `y=0.75` can render with very different whitespace above the figure body if their aspect ratios differ (e.g., colorbar presence or absence affects the rendered figure height). Do not flag a deviation from a sibling figure `y` value without looking at the actual rendered output first. Anchor: cell 31 (Jaccard) of Workstream E review used the same `y=0.75` as the sibling betweenness cell and rendered with more title whitespace; the cause was the colorbar vertical extent, not the y value.

- **Pre-existing row/col label string-coercion in `HivePlotMatrix.from_partition` is cosmetic, not blocking.** `hiveplot_matrix.py:1220` does `group_labels = [str(g) for g in partition_values]`, so integer community labels (e.g. from `louvain_communities`) render as the strings `'0'`, `'1'`, etc. in row/col positions. Confusion risk is low because matplotlib auto-stringifies any non-string label anyway, and the rendered visual matches what a reader expects. Flag as `worth-discussing` if it surfaces during a v0.28+ review; do not block a Louvain-workflow ship for it. The fix (`group_labels = list(partition_values)`) is one line but warrants its own dispatch with regression-test coverage because it touches every existing `from_partition` call output. Anchor: viz-critic re-review of Workstream E amendment 1, 2026-05-26.
